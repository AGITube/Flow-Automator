Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is AGITube Flow Automator?

AGITube Flow Automator is a lightweight Chromium browser extension (Chrome, Edge, Brave) built for digital artists, creators, and studios. It automates repetitive bulk image and video prompt submissions in Google Flow, saving creators hours of manual copying, pasting, and downloading.

Does generation use my own Google Flow session?

Yes. The extension operates securely within your active Google Flow session in your browser. It does not act as a proxy or store your credentials. Generations use your own account allowances, maintaining full privacy and compliance.

Are there any monthly recurring fees or hidden charges?

Never. The Lifetime License is a single one-time payment of $10 USD. There are no subscriptions, renewal invoices, or recurring charges. Once purchased, your license is bound to your account forever.

How do I activate my license after purchasing?

After installing the extension from the Chrome Web Store and signing in with your Google account, click "Get Lifetime" directly inside the extension. Once the payment completes via Dodo Payments, your account is upgraded to Lifetime immediately with no manual keys required.

What is your refund policy?

We provide a 14-day technical guarantee. If the extension does not function as intended on your supported Chromium browser and our support team cannot resolve the technical issue, you are eligible for a full refund. Please review our complete Refund Policy for terms.
